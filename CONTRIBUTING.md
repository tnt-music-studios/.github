# Contributing to TNT Music Studios projects

Thank you for your interest in contributing. This page covers what applies to every repository
under [tnt-music-studios](https://github.com/tnt-music-studios): how contributions are licensed.
How to build, test and submit a change to one repository is in that repository's README, or in its
own `CONTRIBUTING.md` where it has one.

## Licensing of contributions

Each repository is licensed under the licence in its `LICENSE` file. Before your first pull request
can be merged, you accept the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA Assistant bot
comments on the pull request with a link; you accept with your GitHub account, once, and it covers
your later contributions to every repository under `tnt-music-studios` (the bot may still ask you
to confirm on another repository). The bot asks again if the agreement or its form changes. Every
author of a commit in the pull request must have accepted, and each commit's email must be linked
to a GitHub account. The text you accept is the copy CLA Assistant shows you; `CLA.md` mirrors it.

In plain words (the agreement itself is what binds, not this summary):

- **You keep the copyright** in your contribution and can still use it however you like.
- **You grant Timothy Wrona, the projects' maintainer, a licence** to use, change and distribute
  your contribution, including under other terms than the repository's own licence, such as a
  proprietary, closed-source licence. The contribution itself (not necessarily later versions of
  the whole project) is also published under the licence the repository used when you submitted
  it.
- **The licence is perpetual and irrevocable** — you cannot take it back later, and it passes with
  the projects to the maintainer's heirs or a successor company.
- **You grant a patent licence** for any patents of yours that your contribution, alone or
  combined with the project, would infringe.
- **You waive moral rights** against the projects and their licensees, as far as your country's
  law allows.
- **You confirm the work is yours**, you tell us about any part that is not or that carries a
  licence of its own, and you say in the pull request if a generative tool produced part of it.
- **The licence is transferable**, so it can move with the projects, for example to a company that
  later takes them over.
- **Massachusetts law governs** the agreement and disputes go to Massachusetts courts.
- **Your acceptance is recorded by CLA Assistant** (a service run by SAP SE): your GitHub account,
  the form answers, the date and the agreement version.

The agreement exists so a project can be relicensed later without tracking down every past
contributor for permission.

Three cases need a step before you submit:

- **Your employer may own your work.** Have your employer approve the agreement in writing and
  keep that approval, or have them sign the
  [Entity Contributor License Agreement](CLA-ENTITY.md) and email it, with the GitHub usernames it
  covers, to tntmusicstudios2018@gmail.com. Its employees still accept the individual agreement on
  their pull requests.
- **Part of the work is not yours** — code copied from another project, or a file someone else
  made. Before submitting, open an issue in the repository you are contributing to that names the
  source, its author and its licence, and mark the pull request
  `Submitted on behalf of a third party: <name>`. Do not submit it until the maintainer confirms
  how to include it.
- **You are under eighteen.** Do not use the CLA Assistant form. Open an issue in the repository
  you are contributing to and the maintainer will arrange a copy signed by you and by a parent or
  guardian.
